Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Overview

Unlock YouTube Comments provides an independent comment system on certain YouTube video pages. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the extension.

Information processed

The extension may process basic Google account information required for sign-in, such as your account identifier, display name, profile image, and email address. It also processes content and activity you choose to create through the service, including comments, replies, edits, deletions, likes, dislikes, and notification-related activity.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, associate comments and reactions with the correct account, provide notifications, operate the comment system, prevent abuse and spam, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

YouTube page information

The extension may read information from the YouTube page that is necessary to identify the current video and display the independent comment interface. It does not claim to restore or access YouTube's disabled native comments.

Data sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate authentication, hosting, databases, and related infrastructure, subject to their own terms and privacy practices. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users, the service, or others.

Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable rules. User-created content may remain until it is deleted by the user or removed for moderation, security, or legal reasons.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may edit or delete your own comments using the extension where those controls are available. You may also stop using the service and remove the extension from Chrome.

Children

The service is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the extension or its practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the latest revision took effect.
